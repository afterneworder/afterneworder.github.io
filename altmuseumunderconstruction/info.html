If you click "Enter" in the middle of the screen from the current location (or screen) (or homepage), you will be taken to the inside of the museum.

On the inner page of the museum (or inside the museum), there is a picture (alternative text) that depicts the image in the middle of the light gray wall horizontally like a frame. Works are printed in random order each time they are connected.

Click on the image to go to the detail page of the museum's website that houses the work.

If you click the shape of the sign at the top of the screen, you can switch the text mode and view it as an image. (Alternative text is entered in all images. Alternative text can be found through the screen reader or developer mode.)
